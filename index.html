About this prototype & privacy

Speech uses your browser's recognition service, not a custom AI model. Availability depends on your browser, device, language, permissions and network. Try current Chrome on an HTTPS site.

Camera video stays in the on-page preview. This page does not record, upload, store or share camera video or captions. Your browser's speech service may process audio remotely. Clear removes text from the page; it does not delete data your browser's speech service may have processed.

Camera preview is not sign recognition. This is a limited accessibility prototype, not a full ASL translator or a replacement for an interpreter.
